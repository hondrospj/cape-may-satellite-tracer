KNOW YOUR SOURCE

Two dates. The actual imagery.

These are true-color Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A images acquired on October 16, 2025 and February 28, 2026, around 16:02 UTC (12:02 p.m. EDT and 11:02 a.m. EST).

Each date combines two adjoining 10-meter tiles. Zooming closer enlarges those pixels; it does not add detail. Water level, waves, ice, shadows and season can move or obscure the visible waterline. Traces are interpretations, not surveyed shorelines or a measurement of erosion.

Your exports

GeoJSON, KML and CSV contain longitude/latitude coordinates (WGS 84), the imagery date, scene IDs and line length. The browser keeps your traces locally; export a copy before clearing browser data.

PNG includes visible traces, labels and an attribution strip. GeoTIFF and the PNG/worldfile ZIP contain imagery only, resampled to the current view in EPSG:3857. The ZIP includes a projection file and metadata. For original 10-meter data, use the full-scene GeoTIFF links above (large files).
