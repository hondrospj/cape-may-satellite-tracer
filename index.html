KNOW YOUR SOURCE

Two dates. The actual imagery.

These are true-color Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A images acquired on October 16, 2025 and October 6, 2026, around 16:02 UTC (12:02 p.m. EDT on both dates).

Each date combines two adjoining 10-meter tiles. The October 2026 tiles are cropped to the county on their original pixel grid, without reducing resolution. Zooming closer enlarges those pixels; it does not add detail. Water level, waves, ice, shadows and season can move or obscure the visible waterline. Traces are interpretations, not surveyed shorelines or a measurement of erosion.

Your exports

Shapefile, GeoJSON, KML and CSV exports contain longitude/latitude coordinates (WGS 84), the imagery date, scene IDs and line length. Choose Selected line to export one trace, or All saved lines to export them together. The shapefile ZIP contains SHP, SHX, DBF, PRJ and CPG files plus a complete GeoJSON backup. Unzip it and open the SHP in your GIS. The browser keeps your traces locally; export a copy before clearing browser data.

PNG includes visible traces, labels and an attribution strip. GeoTIFF and the PNG/worldfile ZIP contain imagery only, resampled to the current view in EPSG:3857. The ZIP includes a projection file and metadata. For original 10-meter data, use the native GeoTIFF links above (full scenes for 2025 and county crops for 2026).
